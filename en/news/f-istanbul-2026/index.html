Mir Orexov at F.Istanbul 2026

26–29 August 2026, Istanbul Expo Center. We’re bringing Uzbekistan’s nuts and dried fruits — stop by our booth to taste the range and talk business.

Home›News›Mir Orexov at F.Istanbul 2026
27 July 2026

We are pleased to announce that Mir Orexov Samarkand will exhibit at F Istanbul 2026 — Türkiye’s leading food industry exhibition, taking place 26–29 August 2026 at the Istanbul Expo Center (Yeşilköy, Istanbul).

About the exhibition

F Istanbul is Türkiye’s largest B2B platform for the food industry, bringing together professionals from around the world every year. It is organized by So Fuar and has run since 2022.

The scale of the event:

  • 700+ exhibitors and nearly 1,000 brands
  • Visitors from over 100 countries
  • 7 halls, more than 70,000 sq. m of exhibition space
  • Full coverage of the industry: food, beverages, confectionery, food ingredients, packaging and processing technology

Why this matters to us

Exhibiting at F Istanbul gives us the chance to present our products — walnuts, pistachios, almonds, dried apricots, raisins and other dried fruits — in person to buyers, distributors and importers from around the world, discuss supply terms, and strengthen Mir Orexov’s position in the international market.

We look forward to meeting current and future partners at the show to discuss volumes, pricing and logistics for direct supply from Uzbekistan.

Stay tuned — we’ll share our exact booth and hall number closer to the event.
